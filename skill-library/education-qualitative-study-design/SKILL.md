# Education Qualitative Study Design

## Goal

Design a rigorous qualitative education study and prepare data collection/analysis materials.

## Inputs

- Research questions
- Phenomenon/context/participants
- Data sources: interviews, observation, documents, artifacts, classroom discourse, AI feedback texts
- Method preference if known

## Workflow

1. Select qualitative tradition: case study, phenomenology, narrative, grounded theory, content/discourse analysis.
2. Define participants, sampling, recruitment, and ethics.
3. Create interview/observation/document analysis protocols.
4. Define coding plan: deductive, inductive, hybrid.
5. Plan trustworthiness: triangulation, member checking, audit trail, reflexivity.
6. Produce method and findings-section structure.

## Output Format

| Component | Design Decision | Notes |
|---|---|---|

Include:

- Qualitative design rationale
- Participant plan
- Interview/observation guide
- Initial codebook
- Trustworthiness plan
- Findings chapter template

## Quality Rules

- Do not force variables onto qualitative questions.
- Make researcher role and reflexivity explicit.
- Tie every data source to a research question.
